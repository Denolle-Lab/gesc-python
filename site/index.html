Read a velocity-change curve as an inverse problem.

Define the observableThe monitoring operator models direct fundamental-mode phase changes. Coda stretching and MWCS require an additional estimator-specific sensitivity model.

Ask about resolutionLayer sensitivities overlap. Good data fit does not establish a uniquely localized depth change. Examine uncertainty, averaging, and alternative backgrounds.

Separate elasticity from causeTemperature, effective stress, damage, and fluid effects may yield similar elastic changes. Interpretation needs environmental or volcanic observations.
